# Design reference

Follow the supplied Our Story concept for future visual work: charcoal surfaces, warm gold borders and accents, cream serif headings, cinematic Tyria landscapes and two Charr characters. Use minimal navigation, subtle glows and restrained motion with reduced-motion support. Homepage, chapter chronicle and notes should share this style.

The Load chapters button follows this direction. Its JavaScript click handler and /api/chapters route remain the next guided learning exercise.
